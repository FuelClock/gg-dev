Who we are

De Goud Graaiers is a Dutch and English flotilla, about two thirds of us Dutch, all proud to sail under the flag. As a neutral flotilla we keep allied standing with every faction, which keeps our seas calm and our options open. Gerrit’s vision is a fleet where different playstyles and languages converge into one crew that simply enjoys sailing together.

Before you board

Must I be Dutch?

No. We are a Dutch and English flotilla, about two thirds Dutch, and everything here is written in English. The flag is flown for everyone aboard.

Do I need TeamSpeak?

It is not enforced, but it is how organized squads coordinate: donkey.beauty. Just listening in is already a big help.

Is the guild in a faction?

No. We sail as a neutral flotilla with allied standing across the factions, which keeps hostile encounters rare. We stay friendly with the Dutch guilds. If the game adds a Dutch line, or the majority of the fleet wants a pledge, we may pledge together.

What will we actually do?

Everything the sea offers: war hunts, boarding raids, trade loops, expeditions and quiet cruises, in organized squads or chill squads.

How do ranks work?

Cabin Boy is where you start. Sail with us on TeamSpeak weekly and Midshipman shows you every member on the map. Admirals host the voyages and run the fleet. The full ladder is on The Crew.
